Privacy policy

Iris is a private assistant program. Its only user is its owner, and it is used only with its own Google account.

  • It reads and writes Google Calendar, Gmail and Google Drive data only for the Google account that has authorised it.
  • Data it obtains is processed on its owner's own computer, to carry out the owner's requests. It is not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.
  • Access can be withdrawn at any time from the Google account's security settings (third-party access).
